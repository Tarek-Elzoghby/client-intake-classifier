PROJECT: AI-Powered Client Intake Classifier

What you'll build: Extend an Automation Agency Intake Router with an AI classification step. 
When a client submits an intake form, an LLM reads their project description and classifies it into one of three categories 
- simple automation 
- AI workflow
- custom integration
routing each differently: 
- different confirmation email copy 
- different internal notification 
- Google Sheets column logging the AI-assigned category.

Requirements:

Form Trigger collects a free-text "project description" field
LLM node classifies it into one of three predefined categories
Classification result is written to a dedicated Google Sheets column
Confirmation email to the client references their category naturally
Internal notification email includes the AI-assigned category
The workflow handles uncertain/unrecognized classification gracefully (fallback or flag — not a broken workflow)

What "done" looks like: Submit three test forms with meaningfully different project descriptions. 
Each is classified correctly (or falls back gracefully), logged to Sheets with the right category, and triggers the correct email variant — 
all three paths working end-to-end.
